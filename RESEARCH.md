# Planning checks

Checked on 22 August 2026. Booking confirmations and operator apps remain authoritative for the day of travel.

- [ChatGPT Sites](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/sites): hosted, updateable sharing; access can later be restricted and the Site can be deleted.
- [Peach summer 2026 international schedule](https://www.flypeach.com/application/files/8117/7517/7777/20260403_S26_20260329-20261024_int_EN.pdf): MM877 is scheduled HND 02:40 → PVG 05:00.
- [Haneda airline and international-flight information](https://tokyo-haneda.com/en/flight/company_list.html?tab=intFlight): Peach is listed as an international operator. Terminal 1 is not an international departure terminal; confirm whether the booking uses T2 or T3.
- [Shanghai robotaxi development](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-WhatsNew/20260215/e00a579f253d4903a3bdaf6840babc9f.html): operating/testing areas include parts of Pudong and Jiading, but a visitor still needs to confirm the operator and access method.
- [Shanghai–Hangzhou guidance](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-TravelAcrossChina/20250515/fc5c158cf2b141dc8bbc8e7e2fb63256.html): the train journey can be 45–60 minutes, but West Lake is still a full day-trip choice.
- [Wuhan attraction guidance](https://english.wuhan.gov.cn/H_1/NWP/202607/t20260727_2825602.shtml): current Yellow Crane Tower visitor information.
- [Wuhan reservation guidance](https://english.wuhan.gov.cn/H_1/NWP/202407/t20240704_2424356.shtml): Hubei Provincial Museum still requires advance reservation.
- [Foreign-guest accommodation registration](https://english.www.gov.cn/services/visitchina/202603/21/content_WS69ce1384c6d00ca5f9a0a36d.html): a private-home stay must be registered within 24 hours; Hubei is in the online pilot.
- [Hong Kong cross-boundary ferry information](https://crossboundaryferryservices.mardep.gov.hk/): use the live operator timetable for the Zhuhai sailing.
- [Macau Border Gate](https://www.macaotourism.gov.mo/en/sight/border-gate-portas-do-cerco): the Border Gate / Gongbei crossing connects Macau and Zhuhai.
- [Hong Kong High Speed Rail timetable](https://www.highspeed.mtr.com.hk/en/ticket/timetable.html): the published period includes the trip dates; G901 currently serves Ganzhou West → Hong Kong West Kowloon at 19:32–21:56.
- [Star Ferry](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-star-ferry-pier.html): compact harbour option connecting Tsim Sha Tsui, Central, and Wan Chai.
- [Macau heritage route](https://www.macaotourism.gov.mo/en/sightseeing/churches/ruins-of-st-pauls): Ruins of St Paul's and the surrounding old-city sights.
- [Ganzhou heritage bus](https://www.ganzhou.gov.cn/zfxxgk/c144080/202505/a2bc77e990ec425aad1b21f905b3def1.shtml): route 520 links several Song City sights.
